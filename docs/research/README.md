# Research

Tradeoff briefs, one per decision, `docs/research/<topic>.md`, under 700 words. The decision itself lives in the current docs; a brief keeps the options and reasons behind it. If a topic is researched again, its brief is rewritten in place.

Each brief has:

1. **Question.** One sentence: what is being decided.
2. **Recommendation.** The pick and why, in three sentences, with an analogy if the tradeoff is abstract.
3. **Comparison table.** Two to four real options. Columns: fits the need, connected libraries and services, development flow, cost, maintenance and license, reversibility, what it rules out.
4. **Per option.** A few bullets on the facts behind the table, each with its source link.

Check current docs (Context7 or official sources) for every claim about versions, limits, pricing, or licenses, and say when a fact could not be confirmed. Prefer boring, well-documented tools that agents know well; flag one-maintainer or slowing projects.
